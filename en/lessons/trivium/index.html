Algorithm : Trivium (Trivium 流密码). A 288-bit state in three NLFSRs Trivium has a 288-bit state split into three nonlinear feedback shift registers A, B and C of lengths 93, 84 and 111.…

为什么值得读

Trivium targets minimal hardware area: 288 flip-flops, a few AND and XOR gates, no S-box, lookup table or large memory. It is an eSTREAM hardware-portfolio member and an ISO/IEC 29192-3 lightweight stream-cipher standard, suitable for RFID, sensors and gate-count-limited embedded chips. Software can use bitslicing or 64-way parallelism for respectable throughput, but hardware is its main home.…

边界与攻击面

Trivium security depends on the full 1152-clock initialization. Published attacks mostly target reduced rounds, such as cube attacks on 784 or 799 rounds and guess-and-determine attacks on reduced initialization; no practical key recovery is public for the full 1152 rounds, but reduced-round analysis is not permission to cut initialization.…
