By the end, you can explain what “International Data Encryption Algorithm” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : International Data Encryption Algorithm (IDEA 国际数据加密算法). IDEA (International Data Encryption Algorithm) is a 64-bit block cipher designed by Xuejia Lai and James L. Massey.…

为什么值得读

The 1991 paper proposes its preliminary design (called PES in HAC's notes) as a candidate for a new encryption standard: 64-bit plaintext and ciphertext blocks and a 128-bit key, with a design concept of "mixing operations from different algebraic groups" and the goals of confusion, diffusion, and convenient hardware and software implementation.…

边界与攻击面

According to HAC Note 7.107, for full eight-round IDEA, apart from weak-key classes, no published attack is better than exhaustive search over the 128-bit key space; the 2000 draft likewise states that no known attack is better than exhaustive search over the 128-bit key space. HAC considers IDEA's security bounded only by the relatively small 64-bit block length.…
