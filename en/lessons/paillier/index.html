Paillier

Level 3algorithm非对称公钥

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Paillier” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Paillier (Paillier 陷门公钥密码). Addition modulo n² Paillier’s public key is (n,g) ; the private key is derived from p,q and usually written (λ,μ) . Encryption picks a random r and computes c=g^m·r^n mod n² . Decryption uses L(x)=(x-1)/n and computes m=L(c^λ mod n²)/L(g^λ mod n²) mod n . A common choice is g=n+1 .…

为什么值得读

Paillier fills the slot for “private addition without the weight of full FHE”. Private statistics, electronic voting, secure aggregation in federated learning, threshold cryptography and MPC often need only ciphertext addition or scalar multiplication; Paillier provides exactly that layer.…

边界与攻击面

Paillier ciphertexts are about n² bits, larger than RSA, and modular exponentiation is slower. It supports addition but not general multiplication or comparison. The original scheme is malleable and not chosen-ciphertext secure. Deployments should use a hash-bound variant such as Paillier–Pointcheval, or wrap Paillier in a KEM/DEM.…
