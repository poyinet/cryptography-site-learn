Threshold Signature

Level 3algorithm数字签名

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Threshold Signature” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Threshold Signature (门限签名). Shamir sharing and Lagrange Threshold signing Shamir-shares the group secret s into n shares, and any t shares reconstruct it via Lagrange coefficients; FROST uses two rounds—publish nonce commitments, then aggregate z=Σz i into an ordinary Schnorr signature.…

为什么值得读

Threshold signatures turn a single private key into t-of-n: fewer than t shares do not reveal s, and at least t honest parties must be online to sign. They suit exchange cold wallets, CA root keys, enterprise signing and blockchain validators.…

边界与攻击面

The most damaging implementation error is nonce reuse: two signatures sharing the same (d,e) directly solve for a share and then the group private key; RFC 9591 requires one-time nonces and recommends erasing them after signing. Boundaries: threshold signing protects the private key, not endpoints; a malicious coordinator can deny service or choose messages but cannot forge; t or more colluding parties can recover s;…
