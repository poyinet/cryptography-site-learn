Algorithm : Sigma Protocol (Sigma 协议). A three-move public-coin protocol A Σ-protocol is a three-move interaction between a prover and a verifier: the prover sends a commitment a , the verifier sends a random challenge e , and the prover returns a response z . The verifier checks whether (a,e,z) satisfies the relation.…

为什么值得读

A Σ-protocol compresses “prove that you know a secret” into three messages with light computation and communication. It is a basic building block of efficient zero-knowledge proofs and is simpler than generic circuit ZK for discrete-logarithm, RSA and lattice relations. Its three properties answer separate questions: can the honest party pass, can a cheater forge, and does the verifier learn anything.…

边界与攻击面

SHVZK holds only against an honest verifier; a malicious verifier may choose challenges to extract information and needs a stronger zero-knowledge property or a commitment scheme. A Σ-protocol is not transferable by itself: an interaction transcript cannot simply be shown to a third party.…
