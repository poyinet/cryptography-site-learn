Algorithm : Output Feedback (OFB 输出反馈模式). OFB (Output Feedback) turns a block cipher into an independent keystream generator. 为什么值得读

OFB shows a pure keystream construction: the keystream depends only on the key and IV, not on plaintext or ciphertext. This suits precomputation, stream-like processing and settings without block padding, and it makes the security boundary very clear: the IV must be unique. OFB differs from CFB in what is fed back.…

边界与攻击面

Reusing an IV under the same key repeats the whole keystream. The XOR of two ciphertexts equals the XOR of the plaintexts; if an attacker knows one plaintext block of one message, the corresponding output block can be derived from its ciphertext and used to recover the other message's plaintext at that position. SP 800-38A warns explicitly that repeated IVs may compromise the confidentiality of those messages.…
