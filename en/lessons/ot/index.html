为什么值得读

OT is the multiplication gate of secure multi-party computation: with OT, two parties can evaluate a circuit without revealing their inputs. It also underpins private set intersection, oblivious database access, key exchange and privacy-preserving machine learning. Without OT, many MPC protocols remain mere existence proofs. 边界与攻击面

Security depends on the model: semi-honest OT only protects parties that do not actively cheat; the malicious model needs extra verification. A receiver may try to obtain both messages, and a sender may substitute messages; unauthenticated OT does not prove identity. Choice bias, timing and replay can also break privacy.
