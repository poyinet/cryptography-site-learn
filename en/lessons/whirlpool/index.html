Whirlpool

Level 3algorithm哈希

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Whirlpool” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Whirlpool (Whirlpool 散列). 512-bit block, 512-bit key, 8×8 state Whirlpool's compression function is a dedicated 512-bit block cipher W: 512-bit block, 512-bit key, 10 rounds.…

为什么值得读

Whirlpool uses an AES-like wide-trail design: SB, SC and MR provide byte-level nonlinearity and row/column diffusion over the 8×8 state, and 10 rounds spread a one-byte difference across the whole state. A 512-bit digest pushes the birthday bound to 2^256, a comfortable margin over 256-bit hashes.…

边界与攻击面

Whirlpool's outer Merkle–Damgård iteration permits length extension: anyone who knows H(M) can compute H(M pad X) without a key. It therefore cannot serve as a MAC by itself; wrap it in HMAC or use a keyed construction. As a 512-bit hash its birthday bound is 2^256. That is not “post-quantum security”: Grover-style search reduces an ideal 512-bit hash to roughly the square root of its security level.…
