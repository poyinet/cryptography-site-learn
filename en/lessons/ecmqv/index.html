为什么值得读

ECMQV completes authenticated DH in one round and two messages, with only about half an extra scalar multiplication over plain DH; it needs no signatures and no per-member certificate-chain verification, suiting constrained devices. 边界与攻击面

Kaliski’s 2001 unknown key-share attack applies if an attacker can bind its own public key to the victim’s identity, so the victim may share a key with an unintended party.
