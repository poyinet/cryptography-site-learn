为什么值得读

SM9 binds the public key directly to an identity and removes certificate chains; the KGC derives user private keys. It suits email, private networks and settings that want simpler certificate management. SM9 is identity-based; SM2 is certificate-based elliptic-curve public-key cryptography and SM4 is a block cipher. SM9’s trust root is the KGC, unlike SM2’s certificate model.

边界与攻击面

1. Key escrow: the KGC can derive any user key and must be audited as a trust root; master-key compromise breaks the system. 2. Pairing implementation: timing, cache and faults in pairing can leak keys or enable forgeries. 3.
