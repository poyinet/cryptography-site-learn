Rabin Cryptosystem

Level 3algorithm非对称公钥

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Rabin Cryptosystem” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Rabin Cryptosystem (Rabin 陷门公钥密码). Quadratic-residue trapdoor A Rabin signature hashes the message to a quadratic residue modulo n and returns a root of x²+bx≡H(m,u). The public key is n=pq and a constant b; the private factors p,q let the signer take square roots modulo each prime and combine them with CRT.…

为什么值得读

Rabin was the first practical signature family proven “forging is as hard as factoring”. RSA only gives “breaking signatures is no harder than factoring”; Rabin gives the converse reduction from forgery to factors. Verification is one squaring, and signing is one square-root extraction plus CRT, both cheaper than RSA at the same modulus size. That suits smart cards, sensors and low-power verifiers.…

边界与攻击面

A raw square-root signature is vulnerable to a chosen-message attack: the attacker picks random x and asks for a signature on x² mod n. If the returned root is not ±x, gcd(x−s,n) directly reveals p or q. Redundant encoding and randomized hashing are required.…
