Attribute-Based Encryption

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Attribute-Based Encryption” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Attribute-Based Encryption (属性基加密). ABE writes an access policy into the ciphertext or the key. In ciphertext-policy ABE (CP-ABE) the policy sits in the ciphertext and keys carry user attributes; key-policy ABE (KP-ABE) reverses the arrangement. Decryption succeeds only when the attribute set satisfies the policy.…

为什么值得读

It changes access control from “encrypt to one person” to “encrypt to anyone satisfying a condition”. That suits cloud drives, medical records, log auditing and cross-organisation sharing: the sender need not fetch every recipient's public key, and revocation or policy updates can be handled at the key layer. 边界与攻击面

The main risks are collusion—several users combining attribute keys to satisfy a policy no single user could satisfy—plus delayed revocation, overly broad policies and bad bilinear-group parameters. ABE does not hide access patterns; ciphertext size and policy structure can leak information. It provides no authentication and must be composed with signatures, PKI or a trusted authority.
