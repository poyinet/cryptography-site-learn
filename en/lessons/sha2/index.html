SHA-2

Level 2algorithm哈希

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “SHA-2” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : SHA-2 (SHA-2 散列家族). SHA-2 is not one algorithm but six secure hash algorithms in FIPS 180-4: SHA-224, SHA-256, SHA-384, SHA-512, SHA-512/224 and SHA-512/256. Their digest lengths are 224, 256, 384, 512, 224 and 256 bits respectively.…

为什么值得读

SHA-2's lineage is larger digests alongside SHA-1. FIPS 180-2, published on 1 August 2002, supersedes FIPS 180-1 and adds SHA-256, SHA-384 and SHA-512, three algorithms "capable of producing larger message digests"; SHA-1 itself is unchanged as an algorithm, with notation adjustments. Change Notice 1 of 25 February 2004 adds SHA-224; FIPS 180-4 of August 2015 adds SHA-512/224 and SHA-512/256.…

边界与攻击面

SHA-2 is currently a NIST-approved standard hash family; Table 8 of SP 800-131A Rev. 2 marks all six members acceptable for all hash function applications. But "acceptable" does not mean no attack surface; at least three boundaries matter. Length extension : SHA-2 uses Merkle-Damgård iteration, where the chaining value after each block is the input to the next and the final output is the chaining value.…
