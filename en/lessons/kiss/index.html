为什么值得读

KISS means “Keep It Simple, Stupid” and targets a very fast, very short PRNG for simulation and statistical tests, not a block cipher. It has no key, authentication or side-channel goal; using KISS as a stream cipher or CSPRNG mistakes “statistically random” for “unpredictable”. This card also corrects the old catalog label “lightweight block cipher”: KISS updates a PRNG state, and its output is not \(E K(x)\).

边界与攻击面

Rose gives two key boundaries: the 32-bit KISS maximum period is about \(2^{119.5}\), not the earlier \(2^{123}\); about 70 output words recover the state with complexity near \(2^{29.1}\). A long period is therefore not cryptographic security.…
