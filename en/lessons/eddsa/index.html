The retained text opens §1 with the definition: "The Edwards-curve Digital Signature Algorithm (EdDSA) is a variant of Schnorr's signature system with (possibly twisted) Edwards curves." 为什么值得读

The numbered advantages in §1 make the motive explicit. On randomness: "The use of a unique random number for each signature is not required;" no fresh unique random value is demanded per signature. On side channels: "It is more resilient to side-channel attacks;" §8.2 gives the cause, "EdDSA signatures are deterministic." the nonce being derived from key and message rather than drawn at random.…

边界与攻击面

The limits sit at three levels. §8.2 names the risk determinism avoids: "This protects against attacks arising from signing with bad randomness; the effects of which can, depending on the algorithm, range up to full private key compromise." bad randomness could compromise the private key outright — a failure mode that is designed out rather than an attack on EdDSA itself.…
