为什么值得读

FS turns an interactive proof into a one-message NIZK or signature, removing the online verifier and suiting blockchains, credentials, TLS and embedded devices. It lets Schnorr/EdDSA signatures use one hash and one group operation; in zkSNARKs FS compresses a polynomial identity check into one proof verifiable on chain. The saving comes from hashing, not from extra trusted setup.

边界与攻击面

Weak FS has caused real incidents: Bulletproofs, Plonk and Girault proofs were forgeable when the challenge omitted public values (Frozen Heart, CVE-2022-29566). A small challenge space can be brute-forced; omitting the “last challenge” also breaks knowledge soundness of KZG SNARKs.
