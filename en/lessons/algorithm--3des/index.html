Triple DES

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Triple DES” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Triple DES (3DES 三重数据加密). TDEA is not a new block cipher; it uses DES three times as a "cryptographic engine". It keeps the 64-bit DES block and applies encrypt-decrypt-encrypt with a key bundle of three DES keys: C = E K3(D K2(E K1(P))) Encryption first encrypts with K1, decrypts with K2, and encrypts with K3.…

为什么值得读

TDEA was the transition from DES to AES. It reused deployed DES hardware and code and increased key material without replacing the block engine, so 1990s systems could extend their service life quickly. FIPS 46-3 recognized TDEA as a federal algorithm in 1999 while demoting single DES to legacy systems only. It is also a warning about nominal key length.…

边界与攻击面

Two-key TDEA encryption was disallowed after 2015; three-key TDEA encryption was disallowed after December 31, 2023. NIST announced the withdrawal of SP 800-67 Revision 2 in 2023 and formally withdrew it on January 1, 2024. After withdrawal, TDEA is allowed only for decryption, key unwrapping and MAC verification of already-protected data, not for new encryption protection.…
