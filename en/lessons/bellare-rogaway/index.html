Theorem : Bellare–Rogaway Authenticated Key Exchange (Bellare–Rogaway 认证密钥交换). Parties, oracles and sessions The model writes the s-th session of party i with peer j as oracle Π^s {i,j}. Adversary E may issue send, reveal and test queries.…

为什么值得读

Bellare–Rogaway separate “both parties accept” from “the adversary cannot distinguish the session key”. Message authentication alone is insufficient because both parties may accept while the adversary still distinguishes the key; key indistinguishability alone is insufficient because without a matching conversation the key cannot be attributed to the right peer.

1. Model boundary: long-term key compromise, temporary-key compromise, forward secrecy and key escrow are outside this definition; a stronger model is needed. 2.
