Theorem : Shoup's Generic Group Bound (Shoup 通用界). The generic group model In the generic group model (GGM), group elements are not readable integers or rational points; they are random encodings σ(g).…

为什么值得读

In elliptic-curve groups there is no known non-generic discrete-log algorithm; the best attacks are Pollard rho, about √p group operations. Shoup’s bound says that as long as an algorithm does not exploit the special algebraic structure of curve coordinates, √p is a hard lower bound. This is why a 256-bit curve gives about 128-bit security: p≈2^256 and √p≈2^128.…

边界与攻击面

1. Model restriction. Real algorithms can read encodings and exploit field operations, pairings or coordinate equations; none are in the GGM. 2. Counterexamples in concrete groups. Index calculus over F p^ is subexponential; some special curves have faster attacks. The GGM bound does not apply to them. 3. Preprocessing and multi-party computation.…
