NP-Completeness of PARTITION

Level 4theorem理论基础

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “NP-Completeness of PARTITION” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Theorem : NP-Completeness of PARTITION (平分问题（PARTITION）NP 完全性). Decision problem Input a multiset S={a 1,…,a r} with total ΣS=2B; decide whether a subset T⊆S has ΣT=B. The certificate is T: add its elements and compare with B in O(r) time, so PARTITION ∈ NP. Karp reduction Karp 1972 reduces from KNAPSACK.…

为什么值得读

PARTITION is the shortest decision form of “split numbers into two equal-sum groups”. It places NP-completeness, certificate verification, reduction direction and pseudo-polynomial DP on one chain, showing that “the problem is hard” is not “every instance is hard”.…

边界与攻击面

1. Special cases: equal numbers, small B or many duplicates are easy; NP-completeness only asserts the existence of hard instance families. 2. Pseudo-polynomial: the O(rB) DP can be 2^n in the binary length n of B; it is not polynomial time. 3. Reduction direction: reducing PARTITION to KNAPSACK only places PARTITION within KNAPSACK's difficulty; it does not prove KNAPSACK hard. 4.…
