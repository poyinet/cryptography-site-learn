Key Entropy Bound

Level 4theorem理论基础

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Key Entropy Bound” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Theorem : Key Entropy Bound (香农密钥熵下界). The definition of perfect secrecy For every message m, m' and every ciphertext c with P(M=m) 0 and P(M=m') 0, P(C=c M=m) = P(C=c M=m'). After seeing the ciphertext, the adversary’s posterior distribution over messages is exactly the prior; equivalently, H(M C)=H(M).…

为什么值得读

It gives a hard answer to “can the key be shorter than the message?”: under perfect secrecy, no. It explains the length cost of the one-time pad and why reusing a stream-cipher nonce/key is catastrophic: XORing two ciphertexts cancels the keystream and leaves the XOR of the two messages.…

边界与攻击面

1. It applies only to perfect secrecy. Computational schemes can violate H(K)≥H(M) because they do not require an unbounded adversary. 2. The key must be one-time. Reusing the same keystream gives C1⊕C2=M1⊕M2 and perfect secrecy fails immediately. 3. The key must be independent of the message and correctly distributed.
