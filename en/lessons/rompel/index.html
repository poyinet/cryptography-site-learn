OWF Implies Digital Signatures

Level 4theorem理论基础

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “OWF Implies Digital Signatures” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Theorem : OWF Implies Digital Signatures (Rompel 定理). One-way functions suffice for signatures Rompel’s theorem: secure digital signatures exist if and only if one-way functions exist.…

为什么值得读

Public-key encryption needs a trapdoor; signatures do not: Rompel showed signatures can rest on OWF alone. He combined Lamport’s one-time signature, Merkle’s tree and the Naor–Yung UOWHF into a complete EUF-CMA scheme. Hash-based signatures (XMSS, LMS, SPHINCS+) all follow the “one-time signature + tree” path.…

边界与攻击面

1. Very inefficient. A Lamport public key contains 2n hashes and a signature contains n secret strings; a Merkle tree adds log N nodes. Deployment needs optimization or a more compact hash signature. 2. Stateful. A Merkle-tree signer must record used leaves; reusing a leaf breaks one-time security.
