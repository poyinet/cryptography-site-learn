这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Triple DES” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Standard : Triple DES (三重数据加密标准). The two TDEA transformations are printed directly by the appendix. Encryption: “1. TDEA encryption operation: the transformation of a 64-bit block I into a 64-bit block O that is defined as follows: O = EK3(DK2(EK1(I))).” Decryption: “O = DK1(EK2(DK3(I)))”.…
