SHA-3

Level 3algorithm哈希

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “SHA-3” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : SHA-3 (SHA-3 与 Keccak). SHA-3 is the six-function family defined by FIPS 202: four fixed-length hash functions, SHA3-224, SHA3-256, SHA3-384 and SHA3-512, plus two extendable-output functions, SHAKE128 and SHAKE256.…

为什么值得读

SHA-3 comes from NIST's public SHA-3 Cryptographic Hash Algorithm Competition: FIPS 202 states that SHA-3 is based on KECCAK, the algorithm NIST selected as the winner of that competition. The Keccak reference defines Keccak as a family of sponge functions built on the Keccak-f permutation and pad10 1; the choice of rate and capacity determines the security strength.…

边界与攻击面

SHA-3 is currently a NIST-approved standard hash family, but that does not mean there is no attack surface. At least three boundaries matter. Length extension : FIPS 202 says SHA-3 is designed to resist length-extension and similar attacks.…
