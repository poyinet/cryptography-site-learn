SM3 Industry Standard

Level 3standard中国国密

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “SM3 Industry Standard” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Standard : SM3 Industry Standard (SM3 杂凑行标). Padding and blocks SM3 outputs 256 bits. The message is padded with a 1 bit, zeros and a 64-bit length so that its length is ≡ 448 mod 512; each 512-bit block enters the compression function.…

为什么值得读

SM3 provides a 256-bit output, 64-round compression and fixed IV, giving a birthday bound near 2^128 for signatures, certificates, TLS handshakes and HMAC. It joins SM2 and SM4 in the national protocol suite. SM3 is a hash, not encryption; it gives integrity but not authentication by itself, which needs HMAC or a signature.

边界与攻击面

1. Length extension: SM3(secret message) can be extended; use HMAC or hash-then-sign. 2. Birthday collisions: a 256-bit output gives a 2^128 collision bound, not a practical construction; truncation lowers it. 3.
