为什么值得读

It turns lattice cryptography from a collection of constructions into a single analysable assumption. Kyber/ML-KEM, NewHope and FHE schemes trace back to LWE or its ring variant. For this site, LWE is the node that makes post-quantum KEMs, homomorphic encryption and the “quantum-resistant” story intelligible. 边界与攻击面

LWE itself is not broken by Shor's algorithm. Risk comes from parameter choice, noise growth, decryption failure and implementation side channels. Too narrow an error distribution, too small a modulus or a biased sampler can turn the theoretical hardness into an attackable problem.
