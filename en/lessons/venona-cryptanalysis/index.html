Attack : VENONA Cryptanalysis (VENONA 破译). Codebook plus one-time pad Soviet trade, diplomatic and intelligence messages were looked up in a codebook to obtain x i, then superenciphered with a one-time-pad stream k i by digitwise addition modulo 10: c i=(x i+k i) mod 10.…

为什么值得读

VENONA shows that the security condition of a one-time pad is non-reuse. It turns “key reuse” from an abstract prohibition into an operational case: once reused, the OTP degenerates into a statistically analyzable running-key problem. JN-25’s additive book was reused by design; VENONA’s OTP should have been used once but about 35,000 duplicate pages created depth.…

边界与攻击面

1. Correct OTP use is unbreakable: truly random, non-reused, message-length keys defeat VENONA methods. 2. Duplicate pages are rare: about 35,000 duplicated pages are still a minority of huge traffic, and only part of the messages can be read. 3.
