为什么值得读

The design avoids dependence on a secure key-distribution channel set up in advance. The paper calls public key distribution “a different approach to eliminating the need for a secure key distribution channel”: the two parties need no prior shared secret, and can arrive at a common key by exchanging public values.…

边界与攻击面

The paper grounds security in the hardness of discrete logarithms and explicitly treats it as an assumption: “The security of our technique depends crucially on the difficulty of computing logarithms mod q”; if logs are easy, “the system can be broken”. The authors also write: “we do not currently have a proof of the converse” — there is no proof that hard discrete logs imply a secure system.…
