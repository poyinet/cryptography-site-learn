Specification of Datagram Transport Layer Cryptography Protocol

Level 3standard中国国密

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Specification of Datagram Transport Layer Cryptography Protocol” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Standard : Specification of Datagram Transport Layer Cryptography Protocol (数据报传输层密码协议规范). GM/T 0128-2023 defines DTLCP: it adapts the TLCP record layer, handshake family and key calculation to UDP loss, reordering, duplication and amplification attacks.…

为什么值得读

UDP has no TCP connection state or byte-stream semantics, so TLS record rules cannot be copied directly. Cookies let the server validate the source before spending state; explicit sequence numbers let the receiver make replay decisions without a connection. Epoch increments on key change, separating “sequence under the old key” from “sequence under the new key” and preventing cross-key replay.

边界与攻击面

A common error is treating DTLCP as “TLCP over UDP”: DTLCP records carry explicit sequence numbers, fragmentation and reassembly, unlike TLS implicit sequence numbers and byte streams. Cookies stop spoofed sources and amplification, not identity; replay-window size is not a fixed standard value. Certificates, trust chains, revocation and Finished still follow PKI and protocol rules.…
