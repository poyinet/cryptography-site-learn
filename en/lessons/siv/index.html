Synthetic IV

Level 3algorithm消息认证与 AEAD

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Synthetic IV” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Synthetic IV (SIV 确定性认证加密). Synthetic IV and two keys SIV splits the key K into two halves: K1 drives a PRF/CMAC and K2 drives CTR. It first computes S2V over associated data AD1…ADn and plaintext P to obtain a synthetic IV V, then encrypts P with CTR using V as the initial counter.…

为什么值得读

SIV solves the problem of encrypting safely when no reliable nonce exists. Key wrapping, disk blocks and database fields often lack a unique nonce; ordinary GCM/CBC fails catastrophically on nonce reuse. SIV derives the IV from plaintext and AD, so nonce reuse at most reveals that identical plaintext/AD produced identical ciphertext; it does not leak the CTR keystream.…

边界与攻击面

SIV security rests on CMAC being a PRF, CTR being IV-based encryption and correct key separation. Nonce reuse does not leak the keystream as in GCM, but deterministic mode reveals that two inputs are identical; low-entropy plaintext can be enumerated and verified.…
