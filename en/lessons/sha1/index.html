By the end, you can explain what “SHA-1” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : SHA-1 (SHA-1 散列). SHA-1 is one of the secure hash algorithms specified by FIPS 180-4: it takes a message shorter than 2^64 bits and outputs a fixed 160-bit (20-byte) message digest. It processes 512-bit (sixteen 32-bit word) blocks, and its internal state is five 32-bit words H0…H4.…

为什么值得读

SHA-1's lineage goes back to MD4: RFC 3174 says SHA-1 is based on principles similar to those used by Ronald L. Rivest in designing MD4, and is modelled after MD4. Its predecessor SHA-0 was first published in 1993 and SHA-1 in 1996; SHA-0 is SHA-1's predecessor and the two differ only by a single rotation in the message-expansion function, and NIST withdrew SHA-0 in 1996.…

边界与攻击面

A collision is a pair of distinct messages x and y with H(x)=H(y). For a 160-bit digest the generic birthday attack costs about 2^80; SHA-1's collision resistance was pushed below that bound step by step after 2005.
