Algorithm : Linear Congruential Generator (线性同余发生器). The Linear Congruential Generator (LCG) is the most important and most misused algorithm in the history of cryptography. It is one line of formula: X(n+1) = (a · X(n) + c) mod m Four things: modulus m , multiplier a , increment c , seed X(0) .…

为什么值得读

The LCG is not a cryptographic algorithm, but it is a remarkable engineering artefact. Understanding why it succeeded — and why it cannot be used in cryptography — is the fastest route to understanding what "secure" actually means in modern crypto. - It is extremely fast. A modern CPU does one LCG step in a few nanoseconds, an order of magnitude faster than a hash.…

边界与攻击面

An LCG was never "broken". It has always been transparent. Every item on this list is common knowledge, and every year somebody re-learns it the hard way. - The low bits have absurdly short periods. For m = 2^n , the k-th low bit has period at most 2^(k+1) . The lowest bit's period is 2 — it just alternates. Any code still consuming the low bits is, in effect, outputting a counter.…
