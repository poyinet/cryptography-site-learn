MACsec link encryption (IEEE 802.1AE)

Level 3standard消息认证与 AEAD

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “MACsec link encryption (IEEE 802.1AE)” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Standard : MACsec link encryption (IEEE 802.1AE) (MACsec 链路加密（IEEE 802.1AE）). A security header inside the Ethernet frame MACsec inserts a SecTAG after the source MAC address and an ICV at the frame end, optionally encrypting the frame body.…

为什么值得读

MACsec protects link-layer frames, so ARP, LLDP, IP and MPLS all ride under the same protection regardless of whether higher layers enable TLS or IPsec. It suits switch-to-switch, router-to-router, data-centre leaf-spine links and carrier Ethernet that needs line-rate encryption. MACsec defines frame protection only; key agreement lives in MACsec Key Agreement (MKA) in IEEE 802.1X-2010.…

边界与攻击面

The receiver verifies the ICV and drops a frame on failure; the PN increases, and the replay window tolerates some reordering while rejecting too-old frames. The SL field handles short frames, and the confidentiality offset can encrypt only part of the frame body (offset 0/30/50 bytes) when some protocol headers must remain visible.…
