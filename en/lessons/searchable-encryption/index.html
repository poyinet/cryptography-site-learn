Searchable Encryption

Level 4algorithm秘密共享与 MPC

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Searchable Encryption” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Searchable Encryption (可搜索加密). Symmetric searchable encryption The owner encrypts documents and builds an encrypted index. A trapdoor T w for keyword w is derived with a PRF and keys; the server uses T w to locate ciphertext documents containing w and returns them to the owner, who decrypts.…

为什么值得读

Searching in the cloud cannot mean downloading every ciphertext and decrypting it locally; SSE reduces search cost to roughly the number of hits while keeping plaintext keywords from the cloud. PEKS lets a gateway route encrypted mail by keyword without receiving the decryption key; it is one of the earliest searchable-encryption applications and a canonical example of giving a server limited capability.

边界与攻击面

Leakage-abuse attacks recover keywords from access patterns, search patterns and result volumes: frequency matching, query recovery and file injection. Encrypting keywords alone is insufficient; the leakage must be defined and accepted.…
