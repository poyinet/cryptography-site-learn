Grover's Algorithm

Level 4theorem量子密码

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Grover's Algorithm” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Theorem : Grover's Algorithm (格罗弗算法). Theorem (Grover 1996, plus BBBV 1997 and Zalka 1997): finding the one marked item among N candidates takes Θ(√N) queries on a quantum computer — and that is provably the limit. The problem is as simple as it gets: N unordered records, exactly one of which satisfies a predicate f. Find it.…

为什么值得读

Because it takes off only half of an exponent — and because the lower bound proves that half is the floor. The full story of the quantum threat needs both Shor's theorem and Grover's theorem side by side. - Public-key cryptography: a cliff. Shor takes factoring and discrete logarithms from exponential time to polynomial time. Not "a bit slower" — zero . RSA and ECC fall together. - Symmetric cryptography: a step.…

边界与攻击面

Grover's attack surface is not mathematical. It is physical. - Depth is the killer, not qubit count. Grassl et al. (2016) estimate that breaking AES-128 needs about 2,953 logical qubits but a T-depth of roughly 2^80 ; AES-192 needs 4,449 logical qubits at T-depth 2^112; AES-256 needs 6,681 logical qubits at T-depth 2^144.…
