为什么值得读

The CHK transform replaces the identity by a one-time verification key vk. The challenge ciphertext is bound to vk ; a decryption query with vk≠vk lands at a different identity and can be answered under IBE CPA security; if vk=vk , any new valid (C,σ) directly yields a forgery against the strong one-time signature. This is a reduction/construction, not an IBE algorithm.…

边界与攻击面

1. Security model: the underlying IBE is only selective-identity CPA secure; adaptive identity needs a stronger IBE or additional techniques. 2.
