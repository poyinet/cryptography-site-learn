ZUC Algorithm

Level 2standard中国国密

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “ZUC Algorithm” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Standard : ZUC Algorithm (ZUC 算法). LFSR and state ZUC’s LFSR has 16 31-bit cells s0…s15, each in 1…2^31−1 and operating modulo 2^31−1. In working mode the feedback is s16=2^15 s15+2^17 s13+2^21 s10+2^20 s4+(1+2^8)s0 mod (2^31−1), with 0 replaced by 2^31−1.…

为什么值得读

ZUC combines a 31-bit LFSR with a nonlinear F for low latency and small area in hardware, fitting LTE confidentiality and integrity. Its 128-bit key and IV map directly to 3GPP bearer context. ZUC is a stream cipher; SM4 is a block cipher and SM3 a hash. ZUC alone does not authenticate; 128-EIA3 or the upper protocol does.

边界与攻击面

1. IV/counter reuse: the same keystream XORs into multiple messages and leaks plaintext relations. 2. Initialization and state: wrong round count, LFSR modulus or bit reorganisation breaks interoperability and security. 3. Side channels and faults: state updates and S-box access are timing/cache/fault targets. 4.…
