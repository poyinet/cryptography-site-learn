Chi-Square Steganalysis

Level 4attack隐写与信息隐藏

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Chi-Square Steganalysis” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Attack : Chi-Square Steganalysis (卡方隐写分析). The paired-value histogram LSB replacement overwrites the least significant bit of each sample with a message bit. If the message bits are close to random, samples originally in the adjacent values 2i and 2i+1 are mixed; the higher the embedding rate, the closer the two bins become.…

为什么值得读

Natural images do not have independent least significant bits; adjacent grey levels or DCT coefficient pairs are usually unequal. LSB replacement turns a pair into interchangeable bins, so “paired equality” becomes a detectable trace. It requires no key and no message content, only a histogram.…

边界与攻击面

1. Not LSB replacement: LSB matching, matrix embedding, F5 and wet-paper coding do not guarantee equal paired bins. 2. Randomly scattered embedding dilutes a fixed-window statistic, so the original sequential scan may find no clear interval. 3. A low embedding rate is buried in the cover’s natural noise. 4.…
