Canary Trap

Level 2attack应用安全工程

这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Canary Trap” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Attack : Canary Trap (金丝雀陷阱). A canary trap is not about encryption; it is about distribution-side fingerprinting. The same sensitive text is prepared in several semantically equivalent versions with small differences in wording or details.…

为什么值得读

Access control lowers the probability of a leak but cannot answer “who”. A canary trap turns an open set into a finite candidate set and does not depend on catching someone in the act. It supplies attribution evidence, not prevention. In leak investigations, board documents, M&A materials and reporter relationships, its value is turning “anyone could have” into “this copy went only to these people”.…

边界与攻击面

Failure conditions: rewriting or retelling erases marks; copies are photographed, OCRed or retypeset; several people share one copy; n exceeds N and collisions appear; marks are so obvious that they are detected and removed; legal or ethical limits forbid misleading content or inducement. A canary trap also cannot prove who published the material.
