Algorithm : SEED (SEED 韩国内对称加密). SEED is a 128-bit block cipher with a 128-bit key and a 16-round Feistel structure. The 128-bit input is split into 64-bit L and R halves, and the right half R is fed to the round function F with a 64-bit subkey.…

为什么值得读

RFC 4269 says SEED was developed by the Korea Information Security Agency (KISA) from 1998 and is a national standard algorithm of the Republic of Korea, with TTAS.KO-12.0004 (September 1998) as its normative reference. It uses S-boxes and permutations balanced with contemporary computing technology, has a 16-round Feistel structure, and is claimed to resist differential analysis, linear analysis and related-key anal…

边界与攻击面

RFC 4269 Section 3's substantive security statement is only the sentence "No security problem has been found on SEED."; RFC 4010 says SEED resists differential, linear and related-key analysis and is considered secure by ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 27 and CRYPTREC. These are assessments in 2005 documents, not formal proofs against every attack model, and they do not guarantee that no better analysis will appear.…
