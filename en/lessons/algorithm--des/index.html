这一课要解决什么

By the end, you can explain what “Data Encryption Standard” solves, which assumption it relies on, and at least one boundary where it fails.

Algorithm : Data Encryption Standard (DES 数据加密标准). DES (Data Encryption Standard) is a symmetric block cipher. It transforms a 64-bit plaintext block into a 64-bit ciphertext block using a 64-bit key, of which 56 bits participate in the algorithm and 8 bits are parity bits.…

为什么值得读

DES was an early publicly published U.S. federal symmetric encryption standard for unclassified government and commercial data. Before it, strong cryptography was largely the concern of military and intelligence organizations; DES put algorithm details, test vectors and implementation requirements into a public standard that banks, vendors and universities could implement and evaluate together.…

边界与攻击面

The nominal DES key space is 2⁵⁶, or 72,057,594,037,927,936 keys. That number looked large in the 1970s, but became searchable as dedicated hardware and distributed computing spread. In 1998 the EFF's Deep Crack machine completed a public DES challenge in less than three days, breaking the previous 39-day record held by a large network of computers. A 56-bit key is not an acceptable security boundary today.…
