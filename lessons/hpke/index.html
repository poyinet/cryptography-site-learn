HPKE 混合公钥加密

层级 3protocol协议与 PKI

这一课要解决什么

读完这一课，你能用自己的话解释「HPKE 混合公钥加密」在密码学里解决什么问题、它依赖什么假设，以及至少一个会失效的边界。

协议 ：HPKE 混合公钥加密（Hybrid Public Key Encryption）。RFC 9180 §5 说明各模式的共同接口：「All variants take a recipient public key and a sequence of plaintexts pt and produce an encapsulated key enc and a sequence of ciphertexts ct.」混合即公钥封装 + 对称 AEAD的组合。

为什么值得读

动机是既有方案不统一：§1 指出「Currently, there are numerous competing and non-interoperable standards and variants for hybrid encryption, mostly variants on the Elliptic Curve Integrated Encryption Scheme (ECIES)」，并说明「All these existing schemes have problems, e.g., because they rely on outdated primitives, lack proofs of indistinguishable (adaptive) chosen-ciphertext attack (IND-CCA2) security, or fail to provide test vectors.」§5…

边界与攻击面

边界写在安全分析里：§9.1 直说「HPKE does not provide forward secrecy with respect to recipient compromise.」§9.7.4 强调「HPKE ciphertexts are not forward secret with respect to recipient compromise in any mode.」文档另列非目标（不含降级与重放防护、不隐藏明文长度），且未登记任何后量子 KEM 套件。
