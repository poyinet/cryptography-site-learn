事件 ：BB84 论文发表（1984）（The BB84 Paper Published (1984)）。

为什么值得读

论文的动因是：量子测量会扰动非正交态，因此窃听会留下痕迹。摘要写：「When elementary quantum systems, such as polarized photons, are used to transmit digital information, the uncertainty principle gives rise to novel cryptographic phenomena unachievable with traditional transmission media」；…

边界与攻击面

论文的检测论证有明确前提。公开信道「assumed to be susceptible to eavesdropping but not to the injection or alteration of messages」；若攻击者能主动改公开消息，协议需要预先共享小密钥做认证：「The need for the public (non-quantum) channel in this scheme to be immune to active eavesdropping can be relaxed if Alice and Bob have agreed beforehand on a small secret key」。…

这是导读，不是全文。完整定义、来源、计算与图表在主站条目页。
